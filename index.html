    Before you book

    Welcome to Mika Studios! We strive to offer a professional and enjoyable experience for every client. To maintain the highest standards and smooth operations, please review and adhere to the following studio policies.

    Appointments & Booking

    • All appointments must be booked in advance.
    • A 50% deposit is required to secure your appointment. Your booking is only confirmed once the deposit has been paid.
    • Deposits are strictly non-refundable. No exceptions.
    • Our studio address is shared only after your deposit is paid and your booking is confirmed.
    • Late arrivals beyond 15 minutes with no communication will result in a cancelled appointment.
    • Arrivals 10 minutes late will be charged a late fee of R50 per 10 minutes.
    • Our hours are 08:00–18:00. Appointments before 08:00 or after 18:00 carry an additional fee of R200.

    Cancellations & Rescheduling

    • Mika Studios has a strict no refund, no cancellation policy.
    • Once an appointment is booked and a deposit is paid, no refund will be issued for any reason.
    • Clients who miss appointments or attempt to cancel will forfeit their deposit and be required to pay a new deposit to book again.

    Payments

    • We accept electronic payments only.
    • Full payment is due upon completion of the service.
    • Prices are subject to change with prior notice.

    Hygiene & Safety

    • All tools and equipment are sanitized before and after every use.
    • Clients must disclose any skin allergies, conditions or sensitivities before service.
    • Mika Studios is not liable for adverse reactions due to undisclosed conditions.

    Client Conduct

    • Respectful behaviour is expected at all times.
    • Rude or inappropriate behaviour may result in a refusal of service.
    • Clients receiving makeup services must arrive with a clean, makeup-free face.

    Refunds & Complaints

    • We do not offer refunds on any service, under any circumstances.
    • If you are dissatisfied, please speak to your service provider before leaving the studio.
    • Complaints must be submitted within 24 hours along with photographic evidence for review.

    Children & Guests

    • Due to space and safety concerns, no extra guests are allowed unless they are receiving a service.
    • Children are not allowed in the studio.

    Arriving: Lace & Face Preparation Policy

    Clean Lace

    Please ensure your lace is thoroughly cleaned and free from glue, adhesive, makeup, and product buildup before your appointment. Dirty or unclean lace will not be serviced.

    Clean Face

    Please arrive with a clean, makeup-free face to ensure the best results and proper application.

    Thank you for helping us maintain a clean, professional, and seamless service experience.
